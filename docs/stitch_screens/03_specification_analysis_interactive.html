Line Operated Three-Phase Induction Motors (IE-Code) — Energy Efficient

Sectional Committee: ETD 15 (Rotating Machinery) • ICS: 29.160.30
Applicability Rational

Scope explicitly covers low-voltage three-phase squirrel cage induction motors from 0.75 kW up to 375 kW, rated up to 1000V, 50 Hz or 60 Hz, 2, 4, 6, or 8 poles. The subject 15 kW motor falls squarely within mandatory scope.
