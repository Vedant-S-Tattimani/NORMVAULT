### LinkedIn
In public procurement and infrastructure, identifying the correct Indian Standards is not merely a semantic search problem. Statutory compliance with GFR 144(i), BIS Act 2016, and Quality Control Orders is mandatory.

We created NORMVAULT: an evidence-driven procurement intelligence platform designed for government departments, PSUs, and procurement officers.

Key capabilities:
• Specification ingestion & parameter extraction (PDF & Word tenders)
• Deterministic Indian Standards reconciliation (IS 1786, IS 2062, etc.)
• DPIIT Quality Control Order (QCO) enforcement tracking
• Audit-ready Procurement Decision Packages for CAG & CVC scrutiny
