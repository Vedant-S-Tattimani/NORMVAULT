# Brag Plan: NORMVAULT

## What is this app?
NORMVAULT is an AI-powered standards intelligence engine for Indian public procurement that automatically reconciles tender specifications against mandatory Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) specifications, Quality Control Orders (QCOs), and GFR 144(i) statutory rules.

## The angle
Procuring steel or cement for public infrastructure without knowing the right BIS standard is a legal and structural hazard. Retrieval alone isn't applicability—NORMVAULT replaces ambiguous tenders with deterministic, audit-proof Indian Standards verification before CAG audits arrive.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
High-stakes engineering typography in editorial parchment:
"A Single Wrong Standard Can Halt a ₹500 Cr Public Tender."
Instant visual transition into NORMVAULT's authoritative technical parchment drafting canvas.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Tender Spec Ingestion**: Live extraction of technical requirements ("TMT Rebars Fe 500D", yield strength ≥ 500 N/mm²).
- **The Standards Match & QCO Verification**: Deterministic resolution to **IS 1786:2008** with mandatory DPIIT Quality Control Order enforcement highlighted in sage green.
- **Audit-Ready Decision Package**: Real-time generation of clause-level audit trail, GFR 144(i) compliance confirmation, and export package.

## Outro / punchline
"From Specifications to the Right Standards. Audit-ready. Uncompromised."
Closing on the distinguished NORMVAULT seal with "STANDARDS FOR A STRONGER INDIA".

## User flow worth showing
1. **Entry**: Tender specification submitted with ambiguous parameters ("High Strength Deformed Steel Bars - Fe 500D").
2. **Key Action**: Engine matches scope, runs clause reconciliation, detects mandatory QCO order, and rules out superseded citations.
3. **Result**: Full Procurement Decision Package with parameter verification and CVC/CAG compliance audit trail.
