# NORMVAULT Ubiquitous Language

The domain context for identifying, cross-referencing, and verifying applicable Indian Standards for public procurement specifications.

## Standards Language

**Indian Standard**:
The canonical legal identity of a specification published by the Bureau of Indian Standards (e.g., IS 4984).
_Avoid_: Norm, BIS rule, code book

**Standard Edition**:
A specific dated revision of an Indian Standard marking a major technological or dimensional cycle (e.g., IS 4984:2016).
_Avoid_: Version, update, release

**Amendment**:
An official gazette notice modifying or inserting specific clauses in a standard without issuing a full revision.
_Avoid_: Errata, patch, slip

**Clause**:
An atomic numbered section of a standard defining a specific technical requirement, test procedure, or material constraint.
_Avoid_: Paragraph, section, sub-clause

**Normative Reference**:
An explicit, legally binding citation within a standard that mandates compliance with another standard.
_Avoid_: Hyperlink, cross-reference, dependency

**Test Method**:
A specialized Indian Standard that prescribes the physical, chemical, or mechanical laboratory procedure to verify a parameter.
_Avoid_: Testing standard, lab standard, protocol

**Quality Control Order (QCO)**:
A statutory gazette order issued by a Central Ministry making BIS certification mandatory under Section 16 of the BIS Act, 2016.
_Avoid_: Mandatory order, government notice, restriction

**Certification Scheme**:
The administrative framework (such as ISI Mark Scheme I or CRS Scheme II) governing license grants and conformity assessment.
_Avoid_: License type, badge, stamp

## Procurement Language

**Procurement Specification**:
The official schedule of technical requirements, Bill of Quantities (BOQ), or tender document issued by a procuring agency.
_Avoid_: Tender, RFP, inquiry

**Requirement**:
An individual functional, dimensional, or performance stipulation extracted from the procurement specification.
_Avoid_: Item, condition, criterion

**Technical Parameter**:
A measurable quantitative or qualitative variable (e.g. Tensile Strength >= 415 MPa) extracted from a requirement.
_Avoid_: Spec value, metric, attribute

**Specification Gap**:
A discrepancy, omission, or obsolescence in a procurement specification compared to the prevailing Indian Standard or mandatory QCO.
_Avoid_: Error, bug, defect

**Recommendation**:
An evidence-backed determination mapping a procurement specification to primary and allied Indian Standards with confidence scoring.
_Avoid_: AI suggestion, guess, match
